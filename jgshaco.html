Matchup Notes

  • This is one of Ekko's best matchups. Your passive remains on him so you always know which one is the real one.
  • You can pretty much take any E against him. Shacos hardly ever build MR and he'll have to Q away immediately which you can follow with your Q.
  • Shaco has one of the fastest early clears but then falls off dramatically. Don't be concerned if he's ahead early.

Level 1 Ward

Shaco has a few cheese plays but the blue invade is the most common. You'll somtimes see him at your red or krugs after you clear raptors though.

Where to Start

Start Raptors in this matchup. He can't contest them level 1 and hes easy to fight on respawn.
