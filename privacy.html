Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Overview & Scope

LoL Fundamentals ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, and process your data across our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Email addresses, display names, and account authentication records managed via Google Firebase.
  • Transaction, Billing & Shipping Data: Billing transaction records, active subscription statuses, purchase histories, and postal/shipping addresses provided during transaction processing by payment providers. Financial payment card numbers are processed directly by our payment gateways (Stripe and PayPal); we do not collect or store credit card credentials on our servers.
  • Technical & Host Log Data: Basic technical connection logs (including IP addresses, browser specifications, and timestamps) automatically collected by our hosting and security providers for diagnostics and fraud prevention.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process personal data strictly to fulfill operational obligations and provide platform services, including:

  • Authenticating user credentials and providing access to active subscriptions or lifetime account features.
  • Processing subscription billing, renewal updates, payment confirmations, and physical order fulfillment (if applicable).
  • Verifying billing location for tax calculation (such as VAT or sales tax) and maintaining seller protection against fraud.
  • Sending essential account notifications, security alerts, and customer support communications.
  • Maintaining network security and preventing unauthorized access or credential sharing.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

We process personal data only where a lawful basis exists under applicable data protection law, including the GDPR. Depending on the circumstances, these bases include:

  • Contract: To create and manage your account, deliver purchased digital content, process orders, handle billing, and provide support.
  • Legal Obligation: To maintain accounting records and comply with applicable tax and legal regulations.
  • Legitimate Interests: To protect platform security, prevent fraud, fulfill seller verification requirements, enforce our terms, and maintain reliable website operations.
  • Consent: Where we specifically request your consent for a data processing activity, which you may withdraw at any time.

5. Third-Party Processors & Service Providers

We share data strictly with compliant third-party infrastructure providers necessary for core operations:

  • Google Firebase: User authentication management and secure database infrastructure.
  • Stripe & PayPal: Payment processing, fraud prevention, transaction verification, and subscription billing management.
  • YouTube: Standard web embedded video player playback functionality.

6. International Data Transfers

Because we use global service providers like Google (Firebase), Stripe, and PayPal, your personal information may be transferred to, stored, or processed in countries outside your local jurisdiction (including the United States). Where applicable, these transfers are conducted using standard legal safeguards, such as European Commission-approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

7. Storage & Local Browser Data

We use local storage (localStorage) and session state tokens on your device to retain active login sessions and store interface preferences. Disabling local browser storage may limit your ability to access account features.

8. Data Retention & Consumer Rights

We retain account records for as long as your account remains active or as required by applicable tax and legal obligations. Depending on your location, you may hold statutory rights regarding your personal data, including the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent erasure of your personal data.

If you believe your personal data has been processed unlawfully, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority in your jurisdiction.
