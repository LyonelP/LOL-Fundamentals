Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Overview & Scope

LoL Fundamentals ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, process, and protect your data across our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address, unique Firebase User ID (UID), display names, and credentials managed during account creation.
  • Payment Details: Transaction records, billing details, and subscription statuses. Payment card info is processed securely via third parties; we never store full credit card numbers.
  • Usage Data: Basic technical logs such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps collected automatically for diagnostics and security.

3. How We Use Information

We process your data to operate and secure our services, including:

  • Authenticating your user session and providing access to platform features and content.
  • Processing payments, managing active subscriptions, and issuing receipts.
  • Sending critical security alerts, account notifications, and service updates.

4. Third-Party Services

We share data strictly with trusted service providers necessary to operate the platform:

  • Firebase: User authentication and cloud database services.
  • Stripe & PayPal: Payment processing and subscription management.
  • YouTube / Media Providers: Embedded video player functionality.

5. Storage & Cookies

We use browser local storage (localStorage) and session tokens to save your active login session, preferences, and account permissions on your device.

6. Data Rights & Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account exists or as required for legal compliance. You can request account updates or permanent deletion of your personal data at any time.
