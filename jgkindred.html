Matchup Notes

  • Kindred can easily dodge your Q, when jumping on her with E. This also gets her out of auto range. Luckily Dusk&Dawn exists now which I think is a must for this matchup.
  • If Kindred ult's, be careful waiting inside the ult with her. She'll get free damage and can easily dodge your ult damage.
  • Kindred also has the option to build Wits End which completely counters Ekko due to the MR and Tenacity.

Level 1 Ward

Kindred loves to either start your blue, or start her red and then jump the baron/dragon wall to take your blue.

Where to Start

Red and raptors are both good
